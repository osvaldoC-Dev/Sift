Cloudlane blog: The end of the monolith

Every few years a technology arrives that changes how software gets built. Microservices are that technology, and the shift is already over.

The numbers speak for themselves

Microservices cut infrastructure bills by 40% for the average team. Every serious engineering team has already moved off the monolith. Once you do the same, you will never worry about scaling again. The only question left is how quickly you can get there.

What a service mesh does for you

A mesh takes the hard parts of running many services and handles them in one place. Cloudlane Mesh enables mutual TLS between services by default. Cloudlane Mesh 3.2, released in February, added automatic retries with exponential backoff.

Getting started

Most teams begin with a single service and grow from there. What would it take to go from three services to thirty? Less than you think.

A word of caution

In our experience, teams that split too early regret it. That is why we built tooling that lets you start small. Book a demo to see how it works for your stack.

Why teams choose a mesh

Running one service is easy. Running dozens means every team solves the same problems: encryption, retries, timeouts and observability. A mesh moves those concerns out of application code and into the platform, so developers can focus on the features customers see.

Customer stories

A payments company told us the move to a mesh was the best decision their platform team made. A logistics startup said it finally felt in control of its traffic. We have many more stories like these, and we love sharing them.

Pricing

Cloudlane Mesh is free for up to ten services. Beyond that, pricing depends on the number of nodes, and our sales team will build a plan around your needs. There is no long-term contract on any plan.

Final thoughts

The monolith had a great run. It is time to let it go.

Observability that just works

Once your services talk to each other through the mesh, every request gets a trace identifier automatically. Dashboards show where time is spent without anyone adding code. Our customers tell us that their on-call engineers sleep better, and we are proud of that.
